ChatDesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

ChatDesk is a messaging tool operated by Prince Magadzire ("we", "us"). It uses the WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta to send messages to, and receive messages from, people who have chosen to communicate with us on WhatsApp. This policy explains what information we handle and how.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: your WhatsApp phone number and the profile name WhatsApp shares with us, plus any name, email address or notes you give us directly.
  • Message content: the messages you send to us on WhatsApp and the messages we send to you.
  • Delivery information: technical status information from WhatsApp, such as whether a message was sent, delivered, read or failed, and when.

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and provide the service you asked for.
  • To keep a record of our conversations so we can help you consistently.
  • To check that our messages are delivered.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Messages are carried by WhatsApp, which is operated by Meta. Meta processes them under its own terms and the WhatsApp Privacy Policy. We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Storage and security

Your information is stored in our own database and protected by access controls. We keep it only as long as we need it to communicate with you, or as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop messages: reply STOP on WhatsApp at any time, or block our number.
  • Access or delete your data: email us at the address below and we will send you a copy of, correct, or delete the information we hold about you.

Children

ChatDesk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
